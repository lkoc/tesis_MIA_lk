The main study comprises 101 training runs, including eight additional temperature-continuity calibration runs. The frozen subdomain recipe, using 32 neurons in each of 3 hidden layers per material, satisfies the joint criteria in 7 of 21 confirmation runs. The development selection remains provisional because complete acceptance was not achieved. Collocation insensitivity is observed in 0 of 8 comparisons, without establishing universal convergence. A four-mesh 2D FEM comparison finds that redistributing the same power through the skin-effect profile changes the maximum temperature by approximately 0.004 K in the evaluated Aras case; the AC power increase is a separate effect.
